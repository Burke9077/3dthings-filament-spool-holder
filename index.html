04

Print, assemble, and link

The complete pack includes each STL once with its copy count in the filename and manifest. Import each STL, then set that quantity in your slicer.

A · Calibrate

Prove the hardware fit

  1. Download the complete print pack.
  2. Print its nut-fit test with the final material and profile.
  3. Confirm an M3 nut drops into the hex pocket without turning and an M3 screw passes through the bore.
  4. If needed, choose a different fit preset or tune the advanced nut and bore clearances before downloading the pack again.

B · Print

Use the designed orientations

  • 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, 25% infill.
  • Side frames flat, with the shallow rail recesses upward.
  • Crossrails on the broad face, with nut openings upward.
  • Axle horizontal on its faceted flat; add a brim if needed.
  • Caps flange-down; clips flat. No supports are required.

C · Assemble

Capture the four M3 nuts

  1. Place both crossrails with their nut openings upward.
  2. Drop one M3 nut into each of the four hex pockets.
  3. Seat the rail tenons in one frame, then add the second frame with both shallow recesses facing inward.
  4. Install four M3 × 10 mm screws from the outside and tighten evenly on a flat surface.
  5. Pass the axle through the spool, lower it into the cradles, and add both caps.

D · Link

Keep every spool independent

  1. Build and test each holder as a complete module.
  2. Set neighboring frames apart by the configured link gap.
  3. Press one clip over the two front frame edges and another over the rear edges.
  4. Repeat the same two-clip connection for each added holder.

Each module keeps its own axle, so either spool can be changed without disturbing its neighbor.

Hardware per holder 4 × M3 nuts + 4 × M3 × 10 mm socket screws
